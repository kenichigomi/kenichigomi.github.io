About

Kenichi Gomi graduated cum laude from Northeastern University in 2026 with a Bachelor’s of Science in Data Science and Environmental Sustainability Sciences. He has completed two co-ops at Montai Therapeutics and BW Solar as a Data Research Co-op and a Renewable Energy Site Acquisition Co-op respectively. He has a strong passion to analyze energy and environmental data to solve problems.

He is also an avid runner and cyclist. His primary events in track and field include the 800m dash and the mile, but also enjoys the occasional half marathon. Kenichi enjoys bikepacking and camping. In 2025, he and his friends rode up from Boston MA to Burlington VT.
